## Zphase: normal work at exact input end
Claim under examination: a legally enabled bound normal action may progress at pos=end before input release, including across a legitimate yield. Conditional invariant: same owner/response/object/candidate and known input/work cursors, prior reservations and cumulative charges remain; no byte is invented or re-read; a yield preserves the exact next acknowledged action. Basis: Z-A ZA2/ZA3 and input/state READY/YIELDED, together with knownAck, serialDriver, bounded work and stable identity. Induction: initial qualified binding establishes invariant; one acknowledged action changes only its authorized work prefix and charged cost; a yield changes neither completed prefix nor input position. Thus a finite sequence preserves the invariant IF each continuation is legally admitted and effect/ack is qualified. This does not establish that the existing text authorizes that phase: boundary review identifies BND's unproved real-input premise and absent exhaustion rows. A task requiring a future byte is not an enabled zero-byte action. Unknown effect, ownership, budget, or continuation blocks, with no replay.
Counterexample to inference from READY alone: final colon exhausts both input and fuel; READY/YIELDED does not itself authorize Idx nor prove a same-owner continuation or qualified effect. Required evidence: explicit precedence/refinement of BND, identified legal continuation, stable owned input/control, primitive-to-ack semantics, finite charged unit/step/dispatch and parent lifetime. Until separately established Zphase is OPEN for the actual contract.

## Zexit: released input to public waiting state
Claim under examination: after required normal actions, same-input release may lead to NEED_INPUT for known incomplete root or WAIT_EOF for known complete root. Conditional invariant: exact input end, all enabled normal tasks settled, owned carry independent of input, release acknowledged once, and coherent saved grammar/stack/root/tail witnesses. From that invariant, incomplete and complete cases are disjoint; selection of NEED_INPUT or WAIT_EOF preserves the meaning in input/state. WAIT_EOF is not DONE, EOF or handoff. Release-only phase cannot complete Idx or repair grammar. Any remaining byte must still pass grammar/tail processing.
Counterexample: private input is logically released but acknowledgement or state commit is unknown. Neither re-release nor public admission is justified; retain liabilities and block. Another: a consumed closing delimiter with index retirement pending is not a complete root. Required evidence: release-to-ack atomicity/failure and legal post-release state commit, stable carry and alias accounting, close/root witnesses and costs, EOF/finish and owner-loss branches. Z-A explicitly says correspondence and atomicity need proof. Zexit remains OPEN; no Z-B proof or root completion follows from this document.

## WcarryAcrossWait: pending work across later deliveries
Claim under examination: W may retain incomplete key lookup or post-colon index binding across release and wait for a legitimate subsequent nonempty admitted delivery. Under qualified independent owned key/carry, stable same-object generation and known prefix, release of the private input does not change the candidate, decision or completed index prefix. Empty deliveries are admitted and charged but give no index pulse (CB6). For post-colon IdxPending only, CB2/CB3 describe later qualified nonempty admission and bounded index progress before parsing value bytes, subject to all stated qualifications. They do not authorize a pending key-close lookup. Neither case may repeat reservation, comparison credit or acknowledged effects. Induction across arbitrary empty admissions preserves the bindings provided each ownership and budget condition holds. If EOF occurs while the incomplete grammar is drained, finish refuses; it does not complete Idx.
Counterexample: a carry is a view into released input, or its object generation/known acknowledgement is unavailable; later bytes cannot make it valid. An arbitrary number of allowed empty deliveries or no arrival gives no finite wall-clock waiting guarantee. Required evidence: actual independent storage/copy, source and object lifetime, stable decision/view, resource and alias manifest including wait duration, admissible nonempty delivery, finite dispatch/work and failure/cancel/EOF handling. Existing CB0-CB11 address post-colon only, not complete-key lookup. WcarryAcrossWait remains OPEN for actual use.

## HheldClose: classified delimiter remains available
Claim under examination: a legal object-close byte can be held at its actual admitted position while bounded index retirement proceeds, then consumed exactly once before pop/completion. Conditional invariant: pos stays at the same in-range delimiter while retirement is pending, owner/input/alias remain qualified, no later byte is processed, and a legal-close witness refers to the same object/generation. If retirement has finite positively charged progress and each acknowledged step preserves that invariant, after its qualified completion one consumption and one pop may occur, followed by value/parent or root completion under the existing guards. Root tail-only mode begins immediately upon actual root completion; finish still needs EOF/release/handoff.
Counterexample: a delimiter-looking byte in an invalid grammar context is not a legal close; or owner/input is released while the delimiter is held; or a pending close is reported as an already complete root. No retirement/pop permission follows. Required evidence: exact grammar classification, byte stability and ownership through yields, release cursor and effect-to-ack semantics, bounded work/capacity, once-only consume/pop/parent/root/tail correspondence, failure/owner/EOF handling. Event mapping says close/pop timing is OPEN. HheldClose remains OPEN for the actual contract; this is not Z-B or a new selected route.

## C4/C5 refusal timing: decision remains open
Model cases are duplicate key and a decoded-key alias represented abstractly by the same key identity with different span; C5 is not an escape/UTF-8 decoder test; for each RUN2 says Z and WH 24 conditional passes and D=0,18. D is the count of paired runs with differing refusal reason and differing delivery index, respectively: the record says 18 of 24 have different delivery indexes and zero differ in reason. It does not encode direction, magnitude, duration or a universal one-delivery bound. Model source shows Z can perform enabled lookup at end while W's task waits for a nonempty delivery; this explains why a lag can arise. Empty admitted deliveries exist in the contract and CB6 gives no Idx pulse; therefore a general next-nonempty-delivery policy can span multiple numbered deliveries. This is a paper consequence, not a measured count in RUN2. No latency or producer/lifetime bound follows from delivery count.
Existing counter and key/value contracts require duplicate refusal before colon/value parsing; holding and admitting a later delivery without consuming/classifying it is a distinct event whose legal/resource consequences need proof. A next delivery could contain arbitrary later content, be empty, be rejected at admission, never arrive, or be replaced by EOF/cancel. Check receipt/admission charges, body/delivery limits, retention, no future-byte inspection, earlier failure precedence and cleanup for each. The prior C18 decision accepts the duplicate-versus-EOF reason difference only; it does not accept C4/C5 delay.
Decision alternatives: (A) require refusal in the same delivery once key is complete; W needs another qualified way to meet this or is excluded for this boundary. (B) allow waiting only until the next nonempty admission, with explicit empty-delivery, EOF, rejection, resource and lifetime semantics; this is not necessarily one numbered delivery. (C) allow at most one subsequent numbered delivery, requiring a defined outcome when it is empty, absent, rejected or EOF occurs; existing CB6 alone does not supply such an outcome. Each alternative is a prospective policy choice, not a claim of current authorization. No alternative is selected here.

For nested/root and empty/nonempty object, trace fuel exhaustion before classification, during retirement, at final acknowledgement and before consume/pop; include trailing bytes, EOF, cancel and lost owner. Exhibit a valid same-object close witness, admitted unconsumed stable byte, owner/alias and finite work/cleanup evidence. Establish no premature root, no later-byte processing and exactly-once effects. Unknown release/cursor/witness blocks. Deliverable: phase invariants and per-branch source correspondence; missing close or runtime witnesses remain OPEN.

Record numbered delivery, actual key completion, duplicate decision, bytes admitted versus parsed, reason, guards and cost/ownership; C5 models same decoded identity, not the decoding process. RUN2's aggregate difference count does not establish direction or magnitude. Compare the three prospective policies only after these traces; acceptance criteria and any contract amendment require an explicit decision.
